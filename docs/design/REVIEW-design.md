# Independent design review — PRD.md + ARCHITECTURE.md (Phase 3)

Reviewer: fresh-context adversarial subagent, 2026-11-13. Scope: `docs/design/PRD.md`, `docs/design/ARCHITECTURE.md`, checked against `research/SYNTHESIS.md`, `research/tech-feasibility.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md` and `docs/org/*.md`. No other files were edited.

## Verdict

**Not ready to build as written. Revise, then build.** The core idea is sound: a reply tree with computed obligations and symmetric "owes / your move" counters. But four problems would break the product in normal use:

1. **iOS storage split (D1).** The recommended iOS setup (install to the home screen) and the main data path (tap a link in WhatsApp) use different storage. Links open in Safari, and the installed app's storage is separate from Safari's.
2. **Retype and validity (D2).** A retype is last-writer-wins, and the reducer checks every event against the move table. So one retype can silently make the other person's replies invalid, and they disappear.
3. **Ambiguous status rules (D3, D4).** The rules mis-assign or double-count obligations on withdrawn or conceded nodes. They also hide an open objection behind «مُسلَّم».
4. **Gaming and deadlock (D5, D6).** Nothing limits challenge spam, which inflates the other side's "owes" count. "Agree to disagree" can deadlock, with the owing side stuck forever.

The scope is also too large for one session (D20). The glossary library, auto-linking, commitments, Markdown export and most of the "more" moves should be deferred. The time should go to a polished core loop and a correct, fully specified state machine.

## Recommended v1 cut list

**Defer to later (v1.1+):**
- The built-in glossary library (about 20 sourced bilingual entries is a content project) and Arabic clitic-aware **auto-linking**. Keep only `define` plus a plain list of debate terms.
- The commitments sheet (§3.6) and the "shared ground" view.
- Markdown export (JSON/encrypted export is enough for ownership).
- These "more" moves: `refute` (with witness), `ask_source`/`source`/`meta.origin` citations, `granted_irrelevant`, `grant_arg`, the `counter` forms, and the anti-ghaṣb reframing dialog. Keep **`counter`**, **`define`**, **`agree_disagree`** and **`note`**.
- Fallacy tags.
- Relay **anti-entropy republication** beyond a simple "publish my outbox, read all". Also the user-editable relay list UI (ship a fixed, smoke-tested list; editing comes later).
- Retype for nodes that have replies (D2). Ship edit-before-reply only, plus the flagged edit-after-reply (D7).

**Add to v1 (missing for "genuinely useful"):**
- An explicit, single-source validity and obligation matrix covering D3–D8, D10 and D27, with fixtures for: concede-then-object, withdraw with open sub-challenges, a one-sided agree-to-disagree, 3 participants plus a phantom joiner, retype or edit after a reply, and challenge spam.
- The iOS link-to-installed-app path (paste or import inside the app, D1).
- A one-tap «أبلغه» nudge after replying (D13).
- Breadcrumbs plus local text search (D12).
- A participant merge, or counting only participants who have written something (D9).
- A primary «ردّ» flow with the move pre-selected by context (D11).
- An encrypted backup shared as `.txt` (D17), and a «آخر نسخة احتياطية» reminder.
